Section 2 · RFP

About the buyer and the campaign

BCCM promotes India's handloom and handicraft traditions and connects artisan clusters with domestic and global buyers. "Haath Se" (By Hand) is its flagship two-year campaign to position handmade Indian products as contemporary, sustainable and export-ready, and to grow BCCM's own digital channels into the main source of information for buyers, designers and artisans.

  • Audiences: urban consumers aged 20 to 40, designers and retailers, export buyers, artisans and self-help groups, media and policymakers.
  • Languages: Hindi and English, plus Bengali, Tamil, Marathi and Odia for artisan-facing content.
  • Objective: double follower base and triple engagement across BCCM handles in 24 months; build a library of 200 artisan stories.

Section 6 · RFP

Financial bid and commercial terms

  • The financial quote is the grand total for 24 months, inclusive of GST, entered on GeM. A price break-up as per Annexure XI is uploaded in the financial document slot only.
  • Final score = 70 × (bidder's technical score ÷ highest technical score) + 30 × (lowest quoted price ÷ bidder's quoted price).
  • No financial information of any kind may appear in the technical bid. Violation leads to outright rejection.
  • Conditional bids and nil quotes are rejected.
  • Rates fixed for the contract period. No advance. Monthly payment in arrears against approved deliverables.
  • Liquidated damages: 0.5% of contract value per week of delay, capped at 10%. BCCM may terminate when the cap is reached.
  • ePBG of 3% within 15 days of contract. Option clause: quantity or duration may change by up to 25%.
