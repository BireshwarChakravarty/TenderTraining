    01 · PlatformsThe map before the journey

    Where government tenders live

    There is no single portal. These are the places a communications agency like ours should watch.

    GeM

    Today's focus
    gem.gov.in · bidplus.gem.gov.in

    Government e-Marketplace. Central ministries, departments, PSUs, autonomous bodies and many state bodies. Goods and services listed on GeM must be bought through it.

    CPPP

    Central
    eprocure.gov.in/cppp

    Central Public Procurement Portal. Central ministry and CPSE tenders run outside GeM, usually on NIC's e-procurement system with a digital signature certificate.

    State e-procurement

    State
    e.g. govtprocurement.delhi.gov.in · mahatenders.gov.in · etender.up.nic.in · kppp.karnataka.gov.in

    Each state runs its own portal. Tourism, information and public relations departments, and state PSUs publish here.

    Defence

    Central
    defproc.gov.in

    Defence e-procurement for services and the armed forces. Occasional communication and event mandates.

    Railways · IREPS

    Central
    ireps.gov.in

    Indian Railways e-procurement. Mostly works and goods, but zones and PSUs under Railways also tender publicity work.

    PSU and body portals

    Varies
    Each organisation's own tender page

    Large PSUs, banks and regulators often run their own e-tender systems and also post a notice on CPPP.

    Empanelments

    Gateway
    e.g. Central Bureau of Communication (CBC)

    Not a tender, but a door to many: some buyers only invite empanelled agencies, and their rates often anchor government pricing.

    Multilateral

    International
    ungm.org · World Bank and ADB project pages

    UN agencies and bank-funded projects in India. Different rules and formats, with their own registration.

    Tender aggregators

    Discovery only
    Paid alert services

    Useful to spot bids early. Never bid from an aggregator's copy. Always download the documents from the source portal.

    • Ask the room: who has bid on CPPP before? The difference is the signature certificate and the folder-style upload.
    • Empanelments matter for us. Several RFPs we read this year referenced government rate benchmarks when judging cost.
    • Aggregator copies go stale the moment a corrigendum is issued. That alone has sunk bids elsewhere in the industry.
    01 · Platforms

    Why this session is only about GeM

    It is mandatory for most of our buyers

    Under the General Financial Rules 2017 (Rule 149), ministries and departments must procure goods and services that are available on GeM through GeM. Social media agencies, consultants and event services are all listed categories.

    It is where our work is

    Every large government mandate our bid team prepared this year ran on GeM: social media management, social listening and ORM, PR and integrated communication, and media consulting.

    The process is standard

    Every GeM bid has the same skeleton: a system-generated bid document, buyer's additional terms (ATC), the same five participation tabs and the same upload rules. Learn it once and reuse it.

    The shape of every GeM services bid
    1. Bid document generated by GeM: dates, EMD, turnover, experience, evaluation method.
    2. Buyer attachments: RFP, scope of work, ATC, formats, payment terms.
    3. Corrigenda that supersede earlier versions.
    4. Participation in five tabs, then e-sign and submit.
    5. Evaluation: technical, then financial (and sometimes a reverse auction).

    What we cover also applies to CPPP and state portals with small changes: a digital signature certificate instead of eSign, and folder-wise uploads instead of named slots.

    • Keep this short. The point is: one well-understood process covers most of our pipeline.

    04 · EligibilityTry it · enter project values
    
    
    The GeM experience rule, decoded
    
    
    Most GeM services bids use the same past-experience clause: similar completed work in the last three financial years plus the current one.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    
      
    OptionNumber of projectsEach at least
    A3 similar completed services40% of estimate
    B2 similar completed services50% of estimate
    C1 similar completed service80% of estimate
    
      
    You need to meet one option. The RFP may add its own rules: only government clients, a minimum duration, or ongoing projects counted only after a set share has been paid.
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
      
    Sample values from the practice tender. Only count projects that meet the RFP's definition of "similar".
    
    
    
  
    
  
    • Try 1.6, 1.3, 0.9. It fails. Then 1.6, 1.55: it passes on option B. One project's value decides the bid.
    

    


    
  
    
    
    04 · Eligibility
    
    
    Relaxations, exemptions and EMD
    
  
    
  
    
    
    MSE and Startup relaxations
    The bid document says whether Micro and Small Enterprises or DPIIT-registered startups are relaxed from turnover and experience. If you claim it, upload the proof with the bid.
    
    
    EMD exemption
    Under GeM terms, MSE service providers can be exempt from EMD (traders are not). A buyer's ATC cannot take that exemption away.
    
    
    Bid Securing Declaration
    Some buyers accept a notarised declaration on stamp paper (GFR Rule 170) instead of an EMD instrument. We used this route in a 2026 bid.
    
  
    
  
    The warning GeM shows on the declarations screen
    Incorrect or misleading EMD exemption, MSE or Make in India declarations can lead to disablement on GeM without further notice. One wrong click can lock the whole company out of the marketplace.
    
  
    • We will see this advisory on the real screenshot later.
    • If unsure about MSE status for a given bid, ask Finance before answering the EMD exemption question.

    05 · Decision
    
    
    Read the commercial terms before you say yes
    
    
    A bid we win on bad terms costs more than a bid we lose. These clauses appeared across our 2026 RFPs.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Liquidated damages
    Often 0.5% of contract value per week of delay, capped at 10%. Reaching the cap can allow termination.
    
    
    Performance guarantee
    Usually 3% of contract value as an ePBG or bank guarantee, within 14 to 15 days of award, valid beyond the contract.
    
    
    Payment cycle
    Monthly or quarterly in arrears, against accepted deliverables. No advance. Invoices with work-done reports and attendance.
    
    
    Fixed rates
    Rates locked for the full term, sometimes with a fixed yearly increment. Check whether the price is GST-inclusive or exclusive.
    
    
    Option clause
    Buyer can raise or cut quantity or duration, typically by up to 25%, at award and after. Price for that flexibility.
    
    
    Hidden costs
    Dedicated team, on-site days, listening tools, travel and equipment at our cost. Physical presentations with the full team.
    
  
    
  
    • Ask Finance to own this slide in the decision meeting.
    

    



    
  
    
    
    06 · ScoringTry it · change scores and prices
    
    
    How QCBS picks the winner
    
    
    Most of our bids are Quality and Cost Based Selection at 70:30. Only bidders above the technical cut-off have their price opened.
    
  
    
  
    Final = 70 × (Your tech ÷ Highest tech) + 30 × (Lowest price ÷ Your price)
    
  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    BidderTechnical (of 100)Price (₹ crore)Tech ptsPrice ptsFinal
    
        
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
      
      
    
    
  
    
  
    • Point out: Bidder A is the most expensive qualified bid and still wins. A few technical marks are worth more than a big price cut.
    • Drop A's technical score to 82 and watch the result flip.

    07 · Documents
    
    
    House rules for every document
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Content
    • Use the buyer's format word for word. Strike out "not applicable" lines; do not delete them.
    • Same legal entity name everywhere: PAN, GST, incorporation, forms, letterhead.
    • Correct bid number, RFP reference and date on every letter.
    • Validity days exactly as the bid says (e.g. 120 days).
    • Turnover figures identical in the CA certificate, financials and forms.
    
    
    Signing and assembly
    • Every page signed and stamped by the authorised signatory where the RFP asks.
    • Signatory matches the Power of Attorney or board resolution.
    • Index page first, then page numbers through the whole file.
    • Merge in the order of the checklist, with bookmarks.
    • File names like AnnexIV_CA_Turnover_Certificate.pdf.
    
  
    
  
    The rule that rejects bids outright
    No price, rate, fee or cost anywhere in the technical bid. Not in a case study, a CV, the presentation or a sample invoice. Price lives only in the Offer Price tab and the price break-up file.
    
  
    • Every RFP we read this year carried this rejection clause in some form.

    The vault holds audited financials and personal data in CVs. Limit access to the bid team and never share it outside approved tools.

        09 · Submitting
        
    
        Final submit, and what happens next
        
  
        
  
        
    
        
      
        Before you press submit
        • Open every uploaded file from the portal and check it is the final version.
        • Offer price matches the price break-up file and the financial letter exactly.
        • Every mandatory slot filled; every buyer slot either filled or deliberately left with a reason.
        • The authorised person is ready to e-sign (Aadhaar OTP eSign or DSC, as your account is set up).
        • Screenshot each tab and download the bid participation receipt.
        
    
        
    
        
      
        After submission
        1. Representation window: grievances against ATC clauses go in within 4 days of bid publication, before submission.
        2. Bid opening, then technical evaluation.
        3. Technical clarifications: the buyer can ask questions with a short reply window (5 days in one of our bids). Watch the dashboard and email daily.
        4. Presentation and interviews, if the RFP calls for them.
        5. Financial opening for qualified bidders, and a reverse auction if enabled.
        6. Contract on GeM, then the performance guarantee within the stated days.
        
    
        
  
        
  
        • Missing a clarification request is one of the quietest ways to lose a bid. Assign someone to watch the dashboard after submission.

        E1Is Northstar eligible?

        • Experience: option B needs 2 projects of at least ₹1.5 crore (50% of ₹3 crore). P1 (₹1.60 crore) and P2 (₹1.55 crore) qualify. Pass.
        • P4 is ongoing with 60% paid, below the 80% rule. P5 is a private client. Neither counts.
        • Age 14 years, NCR office, 72 employees on EPF, positive net worth: all pass.
        
    
        E2Bid or no-bid?
        Use the scorecard from section 05. Which gates and factors decide it?
        Bid. All gates pass after the corrigendum. Strong strategic fit (craft and culture is a showcase category), realistic technical score around 78 to 82, workable terms. The main risk is the 10-person interview day and the price floor.

        The golden rules
        
  
        
  
          
    
        1. Download everything from the source portal, every time, including every corrigendum.
          2. 
    
        2. Triage in five minutes, decide in one meeting, record the decision.
          3. 
    
        3. Claude reads, a human verifies. No page reference, no trust.
          4. 
    
        4. Every criterion maps to one document, one owner and one date.
          5. 
    
        5. CA certificates and client completion certificates are requested on day one.
          6. 
    
        6. Use the buyer's format word for word, same entity name everywhere.
          7. 
    
        7. Zero prices in the technical bid. Prices only in Offer Price and the break-up file.
          8. 
    
        8. Internal deadline at T-4. Upload by T-2. Never on the last day.
