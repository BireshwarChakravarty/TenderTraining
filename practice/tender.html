Section 2 · RFP

About the buyer and the campaign

BCCM promotes India's handloom and handicraft traditions and connects artisan clusters with buyers at home and abroad. "Haath Se" (By Hand) is its two-year campaign to present handmade Indian products as modern, sustainable and export-ready, and to make BCCM's own channels the first place buyers, designers and artisans look.

  • Audiences: urban consumers aged 20 to 40, designers and retailers, export buyers, artisans and self-help groups, media and policymakers.
  • Languages: Hindi and English, plus Bengali, Tamil, Marathi and Odia for artisan-facing content.
  • Goal: double followers and triple engagement across BCCM handles in 24 months, and build a library of 200 artisan stories.

Section 6 · RFP

Price and commercial terms

  • The financial quote is the grand total for 24 months, inclusive of GST, entered on GeM. The price break-up as per Annexure XI goes only in the financial document slot.
  • Final score = 70 × (bidder's technical score ÷ highest technical score) + 30 × (lowest price ÷ bidder's price).
  • No financial information of any kind may appear in the technical bid. Violation means outright rejection.
  • Conditional bids and nil quotes will be rejected.
  • Rates are fixed for the contract period. No advance. Monthly payment in arrears against approved deliverables.
  • Liquidated damages: 0.5% of contract value per week of delay, up to 10%. BCCM may terminate at the cap.
  • ePBG of 3% within 15 days of award. The contract may be extended by one year on the same terms. Option clause: quantity or duration may change by up to 25%.
