# Changelog

## 3.1.0 · 2026-10-22

- Presenter view never scrolls. A slide too long for the screen is laid out wider and scaled to fit, and fits itself again when answers are revealed.
- Exercises show in two columns and the GeM simulator's upload rows are tighter on the big screen.
- New dark theme: midnight blue with cyan, violet, pink and electric-yellow accents, replacing the warm orange look.
- The site now deploys with GitHub Pages' own Actions deploy, so every push to `main` goes live.

## 3.0.0 · 2026-10-22

- Modern redesign: full-screen slides, a friendly rounded typeface, green, marigold, sky and coral chapter colours, and a clickable chapter progress bar.
- Motion: an animated cover with drifting colour, letter-by-letter title and counting numbers; full-colour chapter dividers; slides that glide in with their content rising in order; flipping index cards.
- Celebrations and sound: confetti on right answers and wins, fireworks on the new thank-you slide, optional sound effects.
- Two links: a presenter view sized for a projector, with a QR code for attendees, Reveal answers, Reset, notes, laser pointer, blank screen and text size; and an attendee view where answers stay hidden.
- Question rounds show one big question at a time in the presenter view.
- New "Thank you, and best of luck" closing slide.
- Fixed tables being clipped inside slides.

## 2.0.0 · 2026-10-22

A rebuild for the first live session.

- New look: an office bid file with folder tabs, red-pen notes, highlighter, sticky notes and bilingual bid fields.
- Almost every slide is now an activity: portal round, index cards, bid search with a pipeline, five-minute triage, Claude mistake hunt, authority ordering, evidence matching, experience rule, stamped decision note, red-pen terms and letter, QCBS sliders, marks sheet, team sorting, backward planner, live tracker, PDF size estimator, rejection round and a two-team quiz.
- A simulator of the five GeM participation tabs that enforces the practice tender's rules, ending in a receipt.
- Real GeM screenshots with clickable pins.
- The practice tender is now a trap hunt with eight traps and a counter.
- Project set-up: folder layout, ESLint, Playwright tests on desktop and phone, CI, GitHub Pages deploy from `main`, facilitator and editing guides.
