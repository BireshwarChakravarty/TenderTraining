01 · Platforms

Why this session is only about GeM

General Financial Rules 2017, Rule 149: procurement of goods and services by ministries or departments will be mandatory for goods or services available on GeM.

Social media agencies, consultants, events and creative services are all GeM categories. Every large government mandate our bid team prepared this year ran on GeM.

Every GeM services bid has the same skeleton, so you learn it once:

  1. A bid document that GeM generates: dates, EMD, turnover, experience, evaluation method.
  2. Buyer attachments: RFP, scope, additional terms (ATC), formats, payment terms.
  3. Corrigenda that replace earlier text.
  4. Five participation tabs, then e-sign and submit.
  5. Evaluation: technical, then price, sometimes a reverse auction.
On CPPP or a state portal?

The same thinking applies. What changes: a digital signature certificate instead of Aadhaar eSign, and folder-wise uploads instead of named slots.

  • Keep this short. One process, learnt well, covers most of our pipeline.

    03 · Scope with Claude

    Claude reads 120 pages in minutes. You check it.

    1. Collect everything. The GeM bid document, RFP, scope of work, ATC files, formats, payment terms, every corrigendum, pre-bid replies.
    2. One Claude project per bid, named after the bid number, with all files uploaded.
    3. Run the prompts in order: summary, eligibility, scoring, checklist, red flags.
    4. Check every line against the page Claude cites. No page number, no trust.
    5. Produce two things: a one-page summary for leadership and the master tracker for the team.
    Tell Claude the pecking order

    Latest corrigendum › earlier corrigenda › buyer ATC › RFP › GeM bid document and GTC. When two documents disagree, Claude must say which wins.

    Never
    • Accept a number without a page reference.
    • Let Claude rewrite a declaration. Use the buyer's format word for word.
    • Upload our financials to an unapproved tool.
    • Use the company-approved Claude workspace only. Tender folders carry audited financials.
    03 · Scope with Claude

    Catch the mistake

    Claude has read the practice tender. Each answer has one wrong line. Find it and open its source.

    • This is the habit we want: every line checked against its source. In a 2026 bid, Claude caught a wrong annexure reference for us. Here, it is Claude that slips.

  04 · Eligibility
  
    The GeM experience rule
    Most services bids use it: similar completed work in the last three financial years plus the current one. Meet any one option.
    
  
    
    
    
      
      Qualifying projects (₹ crore)
      
    
      
      
    Try this
    Change P2 from 1.3 to 1.55. One project's value decides the whole bid.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
      
    The red line is the threshold. Green bars clear it. RFPs often add rules: government clients only, minimum duration, ongoing work counted only after most of it is paid.
    
    
    
  
    
  
    • With 1.6, 1.3 and 0.9 it fails. With 1.6 and 1.55 it passes on option B.
    

    


    
  04 · Eligibility
  
    Relaxations, exemptions and EMD
    
  
    
    
    MSE and startup relaxation
    The bid document says whether MSEs or DPIIT startups are relaxed on turnover and experience. If you claim it, upload proof with the bid.
    
    
    EMD exemption
    Under GeM's terms, MSE service providers can be exempt from EMD. Traders cannot. A buyer's ATC can't take the exemption away.
    
    
    Bid Securing Declaration
    Some buyers accept a notarised declaration on stamp paper (GFR Rule 170) instead of an EMD. We used this route in a 2026 bid.
    
  
    
  
    From GeM's upload page: "In case of providing incorrect/misleading EMD exemption document/MSE/MII declarations for seeking EMD exemption or otherwise in violation of GeM GTC, the Service Provider will be liable for disablement on GeM without any further notice."
    
  
    One wrong radio button can lock the whole company out of GeM. Ask Finance before you answer it.
    
  
    • We'll see this exact advisory on the real screenshot later.

  07 · Documents
  
        The evergreen vault
        Half of every bid is the same paperwork. Keep it current in one place. Click a folder.
        
  
        
    
        
      
        00 Company incorporation, MoA-AoA, PAN, GST, Udyam
        Refresh after any change of address or directors. Self-attested scans, one PDF each.
        
      
        01 Finance audited statements, CA certificates
        New set after every audit. A standard turnover and net-worth certificate saves a week per bid.
        
      
        02 Experience work orders and completion certificates
        One folder per client. Ask for the completion certificate the week a project ends. Keep an index: scope tags, value, dates, government or private, completed or ongoing.
        
      
        03 People CV bank, EPF and ESIC records
        CVs in one standard layout, updated every quarter. They hold personal data, so restrict access.
        
      
        04 Declarations templates for common annexures
        Non-blacklisting, land border, no-deviation, conflict of interest. Always swap in the buyer's own format when given.
        
      
        05 Credentials case studies, awards, sample work
        Outcomes in numbers, no fees. Clear sample work with the account lead before using it.
        
      
        06 Signatory PoA, board resolution, eSign holder
        Who can sign, and until when. Check the PoA hasn't expired.
        
      
        Bids / GEM-2026-B-xxxx one folder per bid
        RFP set, tracker, drafts, final PDFs, screenshots and the submission receipt.
        
    
        
    
        Who can open it
        The vault holds audited financials and personal data. Bid team only, approved tools only.
        
  
        
  
        • The experience index is what lets us answer the experience question in five minutes during triage.

  11 · Practice
  
          Six exercises
          Write your answer down before you open each one.
          
  
          
    
          E1Is Avian We eligible?
          Check every PQ criterion. Watch the turnover period and the experience rule.
          
      
          Yes, but only because of Corrigendum 1.
          
      
          • The original turnover period (FY 2022-23 to 2024-25) averages ₹5.27 crore, under the ₹6 crore bar. The corrigendum's period (FY 2023-24 to 2025-26) averages ₹6.30 crore. Pass.
          • Experience option B needs two projects of ₹1.5 crore or more. P1 (₹1.60 crore) and P2 (₹1.55 crore) qualify.
          • P4 is ongoing with 60% received, under the 80% rule. P5 is a private client. Neither counts.
          • Age, New Delhi office, 72 staff on EPF, positive net worth: all pass.
          • Being an MSE doesn't help here: this bid gives no MSE relaxation on turnover or experience, so both must be met in full.
          
    
          E2Bid or no bid?
          Use the decision note from section 05.
          Bid. All gates pass after the corrigendum. Strong fit, a realistic technical score near 79, workable terms. Risks: the interview day and the price floor.
          
    
          E3What technical score can we aim for?
          Score A1 to C4 from Avian We's profile.
          About 79. A1: six government projects of ₹50 lakh+ gives 7. A2: ₹6.3 crore turnover gives 4. A3: two case studies, 10. B: 23 of 25 with everyone present. C: 35 of 45. Banked marks are weak (21 of 30), so the presentation has to carry it.
          
    
          E4Build the checklist and assign owners
          Every annexure and supporting document, with its owner.
          Documentation: Annexures I, II, V, VII, VIII, IX, XII and the Integrity Pact (VI). Finance: Annexure IV with UDIN for the new FY period, audited statements, PAN, GST, the Udyam certificate for the EMD exemption (Avian We is an MSE), Annexure XI and the price break-up. HR: 10 CVs, not 12, and the EPF list. Account leads: work orders and completion certificates for P1, P2, P3, P6, P7, plus a CA certificate of payments for P4.
