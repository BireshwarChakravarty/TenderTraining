# Facilitator guide

A run sheet for **Winning on GeM**. The full session takes about three and a half hours with a ten-minute break. A short version for two and a half hours is at the end.

## Before the session

- Open `index.html` on the presenting laptop and press `F` for full screen. Press `N` once to check the trainer's notes open.
- Trainees work in pairs with one laptop each. Share the deck link so they can open the practice tender (`practice/tender.html`) themselves.
- Pick a real upcoming GeM bid end date for the timeline slide.
- Have two team names ready for the quiz, and a small prize.

## The short version (2½ hours)

Skip the keyword list, the vault, the tracker and "After you press submit". Give the practice tender and all six exercises as homework, and run the quiz in the last ten minutes.

## Answer key

- **Claude simulator:** the wrong lines are the 10-11-2026 deadline (moved by Corrigendum 1), "private-sector projects count" (PQ-4 is government only), and "hard-copy requirement withdrawn" (the corrigendum never mentions it).
- **Letter:** wrong bid number, wrong entity name, conditional acceptance, 90-day validity, old bid end date, a fee in the technical bid, signed by the wrong person.
- **Practice tender traps:** old bid end date, old turnover period, Annexure V cited for the Integrity Pact, GST-inclusive price, twelve-person team, the hard-copy ATC clause, P4 at 60% paid, and the budget figure in case study CS2.
- **Exercises:** the full answers sit behind each "show answer" on the exercises slide.

## After the session

Send the deck link to the group with the golden rules slide (`#rules`). Ask each pair to send their pipeline of three real bids from the next morning's scan.
